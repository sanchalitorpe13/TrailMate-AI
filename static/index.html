      Open, local and honest

      Open-weight AI, on your device

      Missions are written by Google’s Gemma 3 model running locally through Ollama. No API key and no cloud AI service. Downloading the model the first time needs internet. After that, it works offline.

      Your journal stays yours

      Entries live in a SQLite file on this computer. There is no login, no analytics and no tracking, and this page makes no requests to other websites.

      A safe fallback

      If Ollama or the model isn’t available, TrailMate uses hand-written mission templates. Those missions are always labelled “Built-in template”, never as AI output.

      Honest about its limits

      TrailMate has no live weather, maps, trail conditions or wildlife data, and no GPS. Check conditions yourself, stay on public and safe paths, never eat wild plants, and keep your distance from animals. Progress is self-reported.
